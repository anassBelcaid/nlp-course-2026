Human experts put knowledge directly into patterns, lexicons, and grammars.

Rules: precise but brittle

Why we like them

Transparent · controllable · little data · excellent in a narrow stable domain

Where they break

Coverage · exceptions · maintenance · context · changing language

“The battery is badass.” → ?

We stop writing every rule. The model discovers predictive regularities in data.

A bag of words sees evidence
