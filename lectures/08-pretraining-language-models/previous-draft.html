Conversely, a stronger model’s correct answer would not by itself distinguish memorizing a sentence, combining evidence from many documents, or applying a learned relation. Establishing training membership requires provenance evidence beyond a completion score. Prompt sensitivity is observable; the historical training pathway is not identified by these two runs.
